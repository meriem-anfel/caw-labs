Main Content

Since October 7, the people of Gaza have faced an unprecedented humanitarian catastrophe. Continuous bombings have resulted in the destruction of entire neighborhoods, hospitals, and schools.

According to recent reports, more than 41,000 martyrs have been killed, including over 17,000 children and 10,000 women. Tens of thousands have been injured or displaced, with thousands still missing under the rubble.

The infrastructure of Gaza is almost completely destroyed — 80% of buildings have been damaged or flattened, and the majority of residents are deprived of water, electricity, and medical care.
